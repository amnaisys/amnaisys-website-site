ABOUT AMNAISYS

Where Sovereign Cybersecurity Meets Autonomous Intelligence

AMNAISYS engineers autonomous AI, enterprise cybersecurity and integrated IT systems for Saudi enterprises and institutions that need to modernize operations without compromising control, sovereignty or resilience.

WHO WE ARE

Security, Intelligence and Enterprise Systems by Design

Our name reflects our purpose: AMN for security, AI for artificial intelligence, and SYS for enterprise systems. We bring those disciplines together so autonomous intelligence can operate inside real organizations with the controls, infrastructure and ownership that production demands.

We exist to address a defining enterprise challenge: as AI and automation take on more complex business processes, the defense perimeter has to become equally intelligent, adaptive and resilient.

AMN

Security at the Core

Cyber defense, identity, data boundaries and resilience are engineered into the solution rather than added after deployment.

AI

Autonomous Intelligence

Agentic AI and workflow automation move beyond static tasks while preserving approvals, auditability and human oversight.

SYS

Enterprise Systems

ERP, software, cloud, data, networks and integration provide the operational foundation that connects intelligence to the business.
