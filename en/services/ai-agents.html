SERVICE FAQ

Questions About This Service

How is an enterprise AI agent different from a chatbot?+

An enterprise agent can use approved knowledge, call permitted tools and complete defined tasks across business systems. A chatbot may only answer questions, while an agent can participate in controlled operational workflows.

Can AI agents support sales and customer-service teams?+

Yes. Agents can assist with qualification, product or policy questions, CRM lookups, service requests, follow-up tasks and handoffs, with permissions and escalation rules matched to the business process.

Can agents use internal company knowledge securely?+

Yes. Knowledge retrieval can be permission-aware so users and agents only access approved sources. Sensitive actions can require authentication, policy checks and human approval before execution.

How are agent actions controlled and audited?+

Tool access, allowed actions, data sources, approval thresholds and escalation paths are defined before deployment. Actions and source references can be logged to support review and operational accountability.
