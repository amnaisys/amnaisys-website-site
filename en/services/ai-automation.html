SERVICE FAQ

Questions About This Service

Which business processes are best suited for AI automation?+

High-volume, repetitive and rules-driven work is usually the strongest starting point, especially where documents, approvals, system handoffs or manual data entry create delays. Discovery also looks for exceptions and decisions that still require human control.

Can AMNAISYS automate sales and CRM workflows?+

Yes. Sales automation can cover lead routing, CRM updates, quote or approval workflows, follow-up tasks, customer handoffs and reporting when the required systems expose suitable APIs or integration paths.

Can business workflow automation integrate with ERP and legacy systems?+

Yes. AMNAISYS can connect ERP, CRM, databases, APIs and legacy applications through governed integration patterns so automation can work across the existing technology estate rather than requiring wholesale replacement.

Where do human approvals remain in an automated workflow?+

Approval gates can remain wherever financial, regulatory, customer, operational or risk-sensitive decisions require human accountability. The workflow can automate preparation and routing while preserving explicit human sign-off.
