AMNAISYS — Namecheap cPanel / VPS deployment notes

1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the document root (normally public_html).
2. Keep .htaccess and submit-form.php in the document root.
3. Ensure the mailbox inquiries@amnaisys.com exists and is monitored.
4. The PHP handler sends website form mail to inquiries@amnaisys.com using PHP mail().
5. Confirm PHP mail() is enabled on the hosting account. If the host requires authenticated SMTP,
   replace the mail() transport with the host's SMTP settings before launch.
6. HTTPS must be enabled before production. The included HSTS rule assumes HTTPS operation.
7. After deployment, submit all four form paths (EN/AR Contact and Consultation) and confirm receipt.
8. Do not rename submit-form.php unless the four form action attributes are updated as well.
